How I Work

Good design isn't just tidy screens. It's untangling messy systems.

Over the years, my work has moved way past pushing buttons in Figma. I dive into legacy workflows, bridge the gap with engineering, and use AI practically to move from problem to production-ready design without wasted cycles.

AI-Accelerated Workflows

I don’t use AI to replace critical thinking—I use it to eliminate grunt work.

Whether it’s generating realistic datasets so tables don’t just say “Lorem Ipsum,” pressure-testing edge cases, or speeding through discovery synthesis, it lets me move from an abstract problem to a tangible, testable prototype in a fraction of the time.

Untangling Complex Flows

My favorite projects are usually the ones others find overwhelming: data-heavy dashboards, multi-layered filters, and enterprise workflows with endless states.

I like taking something tangled and intimidating, stripping away the visual noise, and turning it into an interface where users can do their jobs without second-guessing every click.

Production-Ready Design Systems

I’ve watched too many beautiful UI kits die because developers couldn’t realistically build them.

When I build components, tokens, and layouts, I structure them around how code is actually written. It keeps the design scalable, cuts down on handoff misunderstandings, and means engineers don't have to redesign my work in the PR.

Heuristic Audits & Problem Hunting

Before I redesign a single screen, I tear the existing experience apart to see where things are actually breaking down.

I look for the clunky transitions, the misleading microcopy, and the dead ends users silently struggle with. Fixing those hidden friction points often creates more real impact than a complete visual overhaul ever could.

Engineering & Feasibility Alignment

Design doesn’t live in a vacuum. Throughout my career, I’ve worked closely with engineering from day one—talking through API limits, technical debt, and database structures.

Understanding what's expensive to build versus what's simple allows me to design solutions that survive development and ship on schedule.

Full-Cycle Product Ownership

I’ve spent years seeing products through their entire journey—starting from ambiguous whiteboard conversations, moving through rough wireframes and high-fidelity polish, all the way to watching real people use what we built.

I care just as much about whether a feature solves a genuine problem post-launch as I do about how clean it looks on day one.
